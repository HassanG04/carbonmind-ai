# Behavioral and household survey sources

The literature notes identify the public Zenodo record `10.5281/zenodo.10656970` (1.5 Degree Lifestyles) as a potentially useful exploratory survey source. Its public record currently reports no license, so the 5.7 MB workbook is link-only pending a reuse decision. Do not redistribute it by default.

The Swiss Migros loyalty-card data, Chinese household survey rows, UK church-community rows and industrial BOMs described in the supplied paper notes are not public reusable training data. See `../research_papers/README.md`.
