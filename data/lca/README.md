# LCA and product-footprint data

These sources support product-footprint context, equipment lifecycle screening and future Scope 3 exploration. They are not interchangeable with operational energy measurements or national emission factors.

- Boavizta data is useful for order-of-magnitude device comparisons, but its own documentation warns against accurate cross-manufacturer impact evaluation.
- Carbon Catalogue contains product carbon footprints and metadata. Preserve the published values and lifecycle semantics; do not recast them as electricity factors.
- ÖKOBAUDAT is a building-material LCA source and remains link-only until its format, release, license and intended building boundary are reviewed.
- IPCC EFDB remains a factor-discovery source. Retrieved factors require a versioned local factor record before use in calculations.
