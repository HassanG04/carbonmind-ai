# LLM and assistant data

Purpose: structured activity extraction, clarification questions, factor-retrieval evaluation and grounded responses.

The supplied literature notes describe CarbonDialog1K (1,028 predominantly synthetic multi-turn dialogues), but they do not provide a verified public download URL. It is therefore recorded as a research lead only in `../large_data/download.txt`; no dataset was invented or downloaded.

LLM responses must call authorized calculation and retrieval tools. Conversation text is not a measurement and cannot directly create an emission result without validated activity fields, factor provenance and human-reviewable trace.
